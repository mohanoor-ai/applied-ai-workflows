# Venture Desk | Vision, mission and founding brief

## Vision
Give Mohammed a dependable way to build location-independent income from his experience in education, IT, software and data. Over time, the desk should show which opportunities earned money, what they cost in founder hours and which work he would choose to repeat.

## Mission
Help Mohammed answer three questions for any business idea. Who is already paying to solve this problem? Can I deliver a useful outcome profitably with the tools available to me? What is the next action that will teach me whether a real buyer will pay?

## Brief and scope
Venture Desk is a private, reusable workspace for one founder. It supports an idea supplied by Mohammed or a search for opportunities that fit his profile. The first research brief prioritised remote UK buyers, ready-made Google, Microsoft and AWS services, and selective AI-assisted coding for customisation. Other industries and platforms can be investigated in later runs. GitHub holds working decisions, research, tasks and results. Drive holds requested sharing copies. The online school has its own project.

The two planning deliverables are the [evidence-backed offer comparison and 90-day plan](docs/venture-desk/REVENUE-PLAN.md) and the [reusable build blueprint](docs/venture-desk/BUILD-BLUEPRINT.md). The former is a research recommendation. Approval to sell or build an offer is a separate founder decision.

## Goals and success measures
- **Decision quality:** compare buyer evidence, buyer access, time to first sale, startup cost, delivery effort and repeat income. Record contrary evidence and the assumption most likely to overturn a ranking.
- **Commercial feasibility:** model £1,000 and £3,000 monthly profit scenarios, including licences, delivery, selling time and support. Record actual cash and hours separately when available. These are planning scenarios.
- **First practical success:** take one selected opportunity from question to priced offer, put it before real buyers with Mohammed’s approval, and record how their responses change the next decision. A well-evidenced decision to stop is useful learning.
- **Repeatability:** run the same process for a second buyer segment or idea, reusing prompts, sources and delivery lessons. Automate steps only after repeated use reveals where the effort goes.

## How a run works
Start with **Find opportunities for me** or **Investigate my idea**. Both use the same sequence.

| Role or record | Job | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Venture Researcher | Find buyers, alternatives, prices and evidence of purchases. Date sources and distinguish paid transactions, awards, budgets, asking prices and self-reports. | Evidence ledger with gaps |
| Venture Analyst | Compare demand, access, cost, time, risk and margins. Stress-test the strongest offer. | Ranked options and the cheapest useful test |
| Offer Builder | Define buyer, scope, price, demo, exclusions and acceptance measure. | A pilot proposal for Mohammed’s decision |
| Venture Tasker | Turn the selected pilot into ordered tasks with owners and completion evidence. | Action list and decisions needing input |
| Venture Ledger | Record buyer feedback, quotes, cash, hours and costs. | Actual economics and a repeat, revise or stop decision |
| Delivery Playbook | Package an offer that has proved repeatable. | Proposal, setup, reusable components, tests and customer handover |
