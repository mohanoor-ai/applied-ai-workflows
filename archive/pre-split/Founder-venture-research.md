## First fieldwork cycle
Suggested targets for discovery, rather than representative market estimates. Dates start when both founders agree access and availability.

1. **Week 1 | Mohammed and Ali.** Mohammed presents the LMS walkthrough he promised. Ali supplies anonymised counts for a recent month: enquiries, trials booked and attended, first payments, overdue balances and available teaching capacity. Where the month is atypical, compare another period.
2. **Week 2 | Split the evidence gathering.** Interview roughly 10–12 parents across at least two recruitment sources, including families who declined or left. Interview three teachers and three independent centre owners. Ask parents “What did you actually pay for, what happened and which record did you need?” Ask owners “Show me the last time this problem cost you a payment or a pupil.” Obtain a written response from one relevant exam centre or receiving school.
3. **Week 3 | Choose one experiment.** If enquiries stall, trial approved replies. If payments stall, test reminders and reconciliation. If referrals already convert and volume is low, test one source of new qualified enquiries. Record comparable baseline and pilot volumes, paid results, implementation cost and any concurrent campaign. Collections improve cash timing; they count as incremental income only when there is evidence they otherwise would have been lost.
4. **Week 4 | Make two decisions.** For IQSoft, ask whether Ali would pay for continued use and whether an independent centre will buy a scoped pilot. For the school, invite families to a specific paid offer with honest wording on credentials. Proceed when committed seats cover delivery, the teacher is available, and parent onboarding and safeguarding arrangements are ready. If families require an unconfirmed credential, pause that offer or test a different, clearly described one.

### Financial test
For a cohort with a single fee, break-even seats = fixed cohort delivery costs divided by (collected fee per learner minus variable cost per learner), rounded up. Include prep and cover in delivery costs. For illustration only, £600 fixed costs, a £100 fee and £10 variable cost require 7 paid seats. Ten seats leave £300 before wider overheads, founder pay and tax. Compare that with teaching capacity and expected renewals. Price an IQSoft pilot separately using implementation hours, vendor charges and support. Record whether value came from extra sales, recovered fees or reduced costs.
