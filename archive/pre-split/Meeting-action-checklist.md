# Mohammed’s meeting action checklist

The LMS research was your first commitment. Ali offered to lead teacher recruitment, explore Ontario and let you practise on his existing tuition business. The order and assignments below are proposed unless agreed in the call.

## Before the next partner call

- [ ] **Mohammed · Research the LMS.** Compare Teachworks and MoodleCloud alongside the existing Zoom setup. Use one fictional family to test enquiry, trial booking, payment, timetable, attendance, homework and parent feedback. Record monthly cost, gaps and the option you recommend.
- [ ] **Mohammed · Prepare the owner’s phone view.** Mock up the three figures Ali asked for first: classes this week, fees collected and fees outstanding.
- [ ] **Mohammed · Prepare a one-page partner update.** Include the platform recommendation, the walkthrough and the decisions you need from Ali.
- [ ] **Mohammed · Define the income target.** Write down the currency and monthly household take-home you need for the Makkah or Madinah goal. Work backwards through costs and the proposed partner split.

## At the next call

- [ ] **Together · Scope the partnership.** Record each founder’s responsibilities, weekly availability, expenses, decision rights and what the proposed 50–50 split covers. Agree how IQSoft can reuse any pilot workflow.
- [ ] **Together · Pick one pilot.** Walk through last month’s enquiries, trials, paid enrolments and overdue fees using totals. Identify where income was lost and agree one outcome, baseline and review date.
- [ ] **Together · Define the school’s first offer.** Choose the country, age group, subject, weekly hours and intended outcome. Ask prospective parents what they would pay for and which school or authority must accept their child’s records.
- [ ] **Ali · Confirm teaching capacity.** Identify the first teacher, availability, rate, vetting and training process, plus who can cover an absence.

## From pilot to first lessons

- [ ] **Mohammed · Run the tuition pilot.** Use existing tools and owner-approved wording. Track paid enrolments or fees recovered, along with changes in enquiry volume or advertising. Agree any IQSoft case study or paid rollout after reviewing results.
- [ ] **Mohammed · Check England and exam entry.** Get written answers on full-time online-school accreditation, curriculum and faith-school requirements. Ask an approved exam centre about subjects, fees and access in the first target country.
- [ ] **Ali · Check Ontario.** Confirm what a provider may issue at each grade, what authority is required and whether the intended receiving schools accept those records. Record costs and timing.
- [ ] **Together · Cost one cohort.** Include teacher and prep time, software, support and marketing. Set a fee, minimum paid seats, trial terms and a decision date.
- [ ] **Together · Prepare delivery.** Complete a sample lesson, worksheet, teacher checks, safeguarding arrangements, payment process and parent welcome message. Rehearse the whole journey with a test family.
- [ ] **Together · Enrol and teach.** Publish the confirmed offer, book trials, collect fees and deliver the first lesson once the minimum intake is met. Review attendance, progress, parent feedback, costs and renewals.
