# Business plan | Venture Desk
Working proposal · 24 November 2026. Mohammed leads discovery; commercial brand, pricing and partnerships are open.

## Customer, problem and offer
Start with owner-operated service businesses using existing communications and office tools. Ali’s tuition operation is a potential free test site. Map a representative month before selecting a problem. Sell a scoped implementation with optional support if an owner will pay for measurable improvement. Evaluate drafting, follow-up, reconciliation and owner reporting separately.

## Route to market
Start through warm introductions, then approach independent operators with a short discovery interview and a demonstration using fictional records. Ask for the buyer’s last concrete loss, existing workaround, authority to buy and budget. Record proposal-to-paid conversion and maintenance expectations. Obtain permission before using a pilot as a case study.

## Unit economics
Illustration only: a £750 setup at 12 hours valued at £35 plus £80 direct costs leaves £250 before acquisition, general overhead and tax. At 20 hours it loses £30 on the same basis. A £100 support month with one £35 hour and £20 tools leaves £45; three support hours would erase the margin. Track actual hours and distinguish recurring charges, customer subscriptions and one-time work. A free pilot consumes cash and time; cap both before starting.

## Delivery, ownership and scale
Agree scope, access, data use, system ownership and support responsibilities with the operator. Start with an aggregate baseline and a dummy-data prototype. Obtain owner approval for customer-facing actions. Compare like periods, account for changes in advertising and distinguish earlier collection from incremental sales. A paid repeat or second buyer justifies packaging the offer. Reopen Gulf workshops when a buyer identifies a team and budget; retain the FDE portfolio route as an individual option.
