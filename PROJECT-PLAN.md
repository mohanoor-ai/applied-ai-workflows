## Venture Desk mission
Capture ideas, compare buyer evidence and delivery costs, then record which opportunity Mohammed chooses to test.

## Candidate experiment · [AI workflow automation](ideas/001-ai-workflow-automation.md)
Find repeatable problems in businesses already using Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or messaging tools. Build a small improvement and measure its commercial value. Ali offered his existing tuition operation as a possible free pilot. Confirm scope, access, data use, ownership and a review date with him before starting.

| Stage | Action | Evidence to advance |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Ask the owner to walk through a representative month of enquiries, replies, trials, first payments and overdue balances. | An anonymised baseline and one problem the owner values. |
| Select | If enquiries are scarce, test acquisition. If leads stall, test owner-approved follow-up. If payments are late, test reminders and reconciliation. | One metric, comparable periods, cost limit and named owner. |
| Prototype | Use fictional data and existing tools first. Demonstrate the owner’s phone dashboard, approval, access controls and a manual fallback. | Owner can use it and correct a mistaken draft without developer help. |
| Pilot | Run the agreed change. Record volume, advertising changes, costs, enrolments, fees recovered and owner feedback. | Distinguish additional sales from faster collection of existing revenue. |
| Commercialise | Ask about paid continuation and interview an independent second buyer. | A scoped price, implementation cost, support commitment and evidence of willingness to pay. |

## Reusable research
Researcher gathers dated primary evidence; Reviewer checks assumptions, privacy, pricing and alternatives; Tasker proposes owned actions. Use Codex for prototypes, cost models and acceptance tests. Keep customer records and credentials in the appropriate restricted operational systems.

## Opportunity backlog
- Owner reporting, scheduled summaries, follow-ups and teacher-hours reconciliation: choose only after discovery.
- Lesson recap demonstration: start with a fictional lesson; establish permissions separately for private family use and public clips.
- Corporate AI training in the Gulf: interview a budget holder, then test one workshop before building five courses.
- Forward-deployed engineering: produce an authorised, anonymised implementation case study for consultancy or employment.
- Cybersecurity awareness or safeguarding training: investigate each buyer, subject expertise and purchasing process separately.
- Longer-term commercial target: clarify currency, founder time and household take-home ambition before forecasting.
