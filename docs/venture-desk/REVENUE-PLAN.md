# What IQSoft should sell first | 90-day revenue plan

## Five opportunities
Use six criteria scored 1–5: demand evidence (30%), reachable buyers (20%), speed to first invoice (15%), low startup expense (10%), low delivery effort (15%), repeat potential (10%). Scores are analyst judgement. Access means a plausible route to contact, not an existing relationship. We prioritise remote UK delivery and Mohammed’s computing, teaching, deployment and data skills.

Counts = round up ((target + £50) / contribution). Without charging for owner time the setup counts are respectively 2/5, 2/6 and 1/3. Dealer support is £64/client after time; 48 cover £3k plus overhead. Targets exclude tax, holidays, refunds and uncompensated discovery beyond the allowance. Validate actual hours and utilisation. Ten M365 builds require 120 hours including sales, before support; 18 dealer setups require 198 hours. If the M365 build takes 15 hours, its after-time contribution falls to £95. Narrow scope or reprice. A mix of two M365 builds and eight support clients produces £1,032 after the stated time and overhead; acquiring eight retained customers takes time.

## Paid pilot
Offer a £750 fixed-scope, ten-working-day enquiry follow-up setup to a UK independent B2B training provider that uses Microsoft 365 and can show a recent stalled booking. One shared inbox, one owner queue, one reminder, one approved reply template, one weekly exception report, handover and a short defect-fix period. Quote licences separately. Offer £99/month for monitoring and up to one hour of support. Exclude data migration, SMS, LMS replacement, premium integrations and open-ended customisation. Optional AI drafts remain subject to human review and tenant approval; use templates if they perform equally well. Use vibe coding for synthetic fixtures, transformations and reporting; manually test duplicates, permissions, failures and rollback.

Baseline the proportion of eligible enquiries lacking an owner or timely reply. Agree the reply window and target with the buyer. Demo five fictional enquiries including a duplicate, late enquiry and reassignment. Acceptance: every test enquiry reconciles to one record and the manager sees overdue items. Monitor paid bookings separately; do not guarantee revenue uplift.

**First ten prospects:** identify four independent training firms through Mohammed’s professional education contacts or introductions, four London providers with a public corporate course enquiry form, and two from training-industry event exhibitor lists. Check each firm’s recent activity and buyer role; qualify Microsoft 365 usage in conversation. Record ten distinct firms and a reason for contacting each; no outreach has been sent.
**Draft:** “Hi [name], when a company asks about training dates or a group booking, how do you see who owns the reply? I’ve put together a short Microsoft 365 demo showing overdue enquiries and the next action. If missed follow-ups are a live problem, could we walk through one anonymised example? I offer a fixed £750 pilot for one inbox.”

## 90 days
| When | Action and decision |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Define ideal buyer, hours available, walk-away price and success measure. |
| Day 2 | Build five fictional enquiry examples; storyboard owner and manager views. |
| Day 3 | Configure the minimal template, rehearse failures and record a three-minute demo. |
| Day 4 | Qualify ten named prospects and identify the person responsible for bookings. |
| Day 5 | Send five individually reviewed approaches through appropriate channels; log results. |
| Day 6 | Approach the next five and conduct any booked discovery calls. |
| Day 7 | Review objections; issue a written fixed-scope quote where the buyer can show a real problem. |
| By day 30 | Aim for 20 qualified approaches, five conversations and one paid pilot. With fewer than three conversations, change access/channel. With five substantive conversations and no costly problem, test dealer discovery before further building. |
| By day 60 | Deliver the pilot if bought, record actual hours, faults, payments and buyer feedback. Seek an independent second buyer. If delivery exceeds 12 build hours or requires unsupported connectors, re-scope before the next quote. |
| By day 90 | Target three paid implementations and one voluntary support renewal. Publish an authorised case study and reusable playbook only with evidence. After ten qualified conversations and three credible quotes with no sale, record the reasons and revise price/scope or stop. |

Targets are operating hypotheses. Review against cash collected and economic profit.
