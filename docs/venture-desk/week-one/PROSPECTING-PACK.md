# Week one | Corporate training enquiry pilot
Decision: validate demand before building a client integration. Mohammed owns this run. The ten organisations below are candidates from public pages checked 24 November 2026. Their buying interest, inbox technology, enquiry volumes and budgets are unknown. No external contact has been made.

## Run the three-minute demo
Download [the offline demo](enquiry-demo.html) and open it in a browser. It runs with fictional data only. It neither connects to Microsoft 365 nor sends email.
1. Five incoming events resolve to four enquiries because E02 appears twice. One enquiry is unassigned and one overdue. The clock is fixed for this demonstration.
2. Select Needs attention. Assign E01 to Sam. E01 leaves the attention queue. Record a reply for E02. The overdue count falls to zero.
3. Return to All and reassign E04 to Sam. Ask the buyer to locate the next outstanding reply. Reset the demo before the next conversation.
Production acceptance would additionally require testing actual message IDs, working calendars, access, failures, and recovery inside the buyer’s authorised tenant.

## Buyer conversation
Start with WeAreExcel and Keys because both publish a group-booking route. Teaching Microsoft products is not proof of internal Microsoft 365 use. Use an introduction or an appropriate business contact route.
- “Talk me through the last group quote that went quiet. How did you discover it, and was the booking recovered?”
- “How many eligible enquiries and late first replies did you have last month? Is another system already chasing them?”
- “Who owns the inbox and approves a £750 pilot? What would make the result worth paying for?”
- Only demo if relevant. Log stated problem, baseline availability, existing tool, decision-maker role, next step and reason for declining.

## Outreach and offer draft
“Hi [name], I saw that you arrange [specific in-house course]. When a group booking request is passed between colleagues, how do you check who owns the reply? I have a three-minute example using fictional enquiries. If this has caused a missed booking recently, would a short walkthrough be useful? Mohammed, Venture Desk.”

Proposed quote: £750 for one shared inbox, owner queue, reviewable reminder, weekly exception report, handover and 14 days of defect fixes. Confirm licence costs, permissions, applicable VAT, start date and buyer-defined reply window before signing. No migration, bulk messaging or guaranteed sales uplift. Optional £99/month monitoring with up to one support hour. Request buyer approval before sending anything from their tenant.

## Decision and economics
£750 minus £25 direct costs minus 12 total hours at £35 = £305 per setup before shared overhead and tax. Four setups produce £1,170 after £50 monthly overhead; ten produce £3,000 exactly. If build + sales takes 18 hours, contribution falls to £95. Record actual time, payment and comparable baseline before calling the offer profitable. After five substantive interviews without a costly problem, move the next discovery test to Dealer Desk.
